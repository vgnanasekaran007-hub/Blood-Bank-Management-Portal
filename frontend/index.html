Medical Criteria Checksheet

Before registration, verify that you meet the National Blood Transfusion Council (NBTC) clinical standards:

  • ✓ Age: 18 – 65 years old
  • ✓ Weight: Minimum 45 kg (whole blood donation)
  • ✓ Safe Interval: Minimum 90 days (3 months) since last whole blood donation
  • ✓ Health: Normal temperature, blood pressure, and free from acute respiratory infections
  • ✓ Data Security: Phone number is never published openly. Encrypted and stored safely.
Real-time Database Storage Submitting this checksheet creates or updates live rows in the bloodbank_donorprofile and auth_user tables. Real-time counters immediately refresh.

Inventory Integrity & Transaction Rules

🔒 Non-Negative Constraint Every inventory deduction checks units_available >= units_needed at the database level. Stock can never go below zero.
✅ Double Verification Rule Donor submissions do not automatically increment stock. Units enter inventory only after staff verification.
⚠️ Low Stock Alert Any group below the minimum threshold (5 units) triggers an automated critical alert in the live dispatch ticker.
